# Master Graphic Image Assets: Traditional Paite Puan Patterns

*Skill Author: **Casey Paite***  
*Digital reproductions by **Mr. Siam Naulak**, Naulak Web Design ([naulak.com](https://naulak.com)), created as design material for the **2027 Calendar of the Young Paite Association (YPA), West Delhi Unit**.*

Authoritative master reproductions of the 12 canonical Paite Puan (woven cloth) designs of the Paite (Zo) people.

---

## Zero-Variation Cultural Mandate

These designs are sacred, living cultural heritage. **They are NOT subject to arbitrary variations, recoloring, or alterations.** Every rendering, generation, description, or physical weave MUST strictly adhere to:
1. **Canonical Pattern Architecture & Symmetry** (bands, dividers, stepped loom geometry, fringes on side edges only).
2. **Exact Color Ratios & Mathematical Proportions** (relative band thicknesses, core-to-border ratios, interval spacing).
3. **Strict Color Sequence & Spatial Order** (top-to-bottom and inner-to-outer layer order).
