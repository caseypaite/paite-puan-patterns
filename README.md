## The Zero-Variation Mandate

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Traditional Paite puan patterns are sacred living cultural heritage and are NOT subject to variations.**  
> Any AI generation, graphic rendering, physical weaving specification, or analysis called a Paite puan MUST strictly follow the canonical pattern geometry, exact mathematical color ratios, and sequence order without modern recoloring or alteration.

### The Three Pillars of Paite Puan Fidelity:
1. **Loom Geometry:** Discrete warp/weft grid structure, stepped/pixelated handloom motifs (never curved or printed), flat-lay orthographic framing, and fringes exclusively on the far left/right vertical edges.
2. **Mathematical Color Ratios:** Fixed proportional relationships (e.g. 3:1 band core to border in Puandum and Siamsin; 1.5× black ground intervals; 1:2:2:1 vertical stripe ratios; 50/50 split in Aksi).
3. **Chromatic Sequence Order:** Inward color progression (e.g. Siamsin composite bands: *white hairline $\rightarrow$ royal blue $\rightarrow$ kelly green $\rightarrow$ hot-pink core $\rightarrow$ kelly green $\rightarrow$ royal blue $\rightarrow$ white hairline*, where green strictly buffers blue from pink; zero yellow).

## Quick Installation

### Option 1: Via Skills Package Manager (Recommended)
```bash
npx skills add caseypaite/paite-puan-patterns --agent antigravity
```

### Option 2: Clone Directly into Global Antigravity Config
```bash
git clone https://github.com/caseypaite/paite-puan-patterns.git ~/.gemini/config/skills/paite-puan-patterns
```

### Option 3: Add to a Project Workspace
```bash
mkdir -p .agents/skills
git clone https://github.com/caseypaite/paite-puan-patterns.git .agents/skills/paite-puan-patterns
```

## The 12 Canonical Paite Puan Designs

| # | Pattern Name | Ground Color | Iconic Elements & Mathematical Ratios | Master Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Paite Puandum** | Jet Black `#000000` | 5 crimson horizontal bands bordered in lemon yellow & emerald green (3:1 core ratio); 4 vertical stripes (1:2:2:1 ratio); white hairlines with green-magenta-blue lozenges. | `puan-images/01_Paite_Puandum.jpeg` |
| **02** | **Siamsin Puan** | Pitch Black `#000000` | 5 composite bands with strict 7-layer order (white $\rightarrow$ royal blue $\rightarrow$ kelly green $\rightarrow$ wide hot-pink core $\rightarrow$ green $\rightarrow$ blue $\rightarrow$ white); green buffers blue from pink; 10 lozenges per line; zero yellow. | `puan-images/02_Siamsin_Puan.jpeg` |
| **03** | **Thangsuah Puan** | Deep Black `#000000` | 5 crimson bands with central white line of 8 lozenges (blue $\rightarrow$ pink $\rightarrow$ green); black intervals holding 3 hairlines in 1:1:1 spacing (red $\rightarrow$ white $\rightarrow$ red). | `puan-images/03_Thangsuah_Puan.jpeg` |
| **04** | **Puanlaisan** | Crimson `#B81428` | 9 mirror-symmetric horizontal zones; crimson headers with geometric trim, white bands, and wide central Laisan core with 3 motif lines of 9 tri-colour lozenges each. | `puan-images/04_Puanlaisan.jpeg` |
| **05** | **Galhang Puan** | Pitch Black `#000000` | 11 horizontal white lines (braided rope-twists at 1, 5, 9; beaded chains at 3, 7, 11; solid white at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10); vertical edge column in amber, orange, crimson, and zigzag. | `puan-images/05_Galhang_Puan.jpeg` |
| **06** | **Khephiau Puan** | Pitch Black `#000000` | Crisp monochrome 9×7 orthogonal windowpane plaid grid; grey basketweave twill cores flanked by thin white lines; midway triple white pinstripes. | `puan-images/06_Khephiau_Puan.jpeg` |
| **07** | **Ngoutekhelh Puan** | Pitch Black `#000000` | 3 equal central panels divided by 2 solid black columns; 16 white bands separated by 15 thin black stripes (3:1 width ratio); black top/bottom borders with 5 white stripes. | `puan-images/07_Ngoutekhelh_Puan.jpeg` |
| **08** | **Mal-am Puan** | Pitch Black `#000000` | Purely vertical architectural structure: 4 wide panels with upper 88% dense interlocking triangle/pinwheel lattice transitioning cleanly into fine herringbone twill at base. | `puan-images/08_Mal-am_Puan.jpeg` |
| **09** | **Aksi Puan** | Midnight Indigo `#030322` | Exact 50/50 horizontal split: upper half plain indigo; lower half filled with 18 offset rows of crisp stepped 8-point white star rosettes (*Aksi*). | `puan-images/09_Aksi_Puan.jpeg` |
| **10** | **Pangpalek Puan** | Midnight Indigo `#06051A` | Upper 78% plain; lower horizontal decorative zone holding 27 white butterfly motifs (*Pangpalek*) flanking 26 interlocking stepped concentric diamonds in crimson, orange, green, and white. | `puan-images/10_Pangpalek_Puan.jpeg` |
| **11** | **Lianbuangthuam Puan** | Deep Maroon `#61071C` | Plain top/bottom maroon borders (12% each); 12 multicolored central bands alternating diamond-lattice with white crosses and stepped diagonal chevrons; white side margins. | `puan-images/11_Lianbuangthuam_Puan.jpeg` |
| **12** | **Puanthupi Puan** | Deep Burgundy `#4A0414` | Solid black header (11% height); 7 stacked bands alternating 4 multi-tiered Bargello flame-stitch sawtooth chevron bands and 3 diamond argyle lattice bands; 3-hairline dividers. | `puan-images/12_Puanthupi_Puan.jpeg` |
